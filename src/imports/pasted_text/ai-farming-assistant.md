Act as a Senior UX/UI Designer, Senior Product Designer, AI Product Architect, and Full-Stack Software Engineer with 20+ years of experience.

Your task is to design a professional, modern, hackathon-winning prototype for an AI-powered Cropzen.

The project title is:

"AI-Based Crop Recommendation System for Farmers"

However, this is NOT just a crop recommendation app.

The core mission is:

"Break the language barrier between farmers and agricultural technology."

The application should allow every farmer to interact with AI using their own language and voice.

The design should look like a startup product ready for investors.
